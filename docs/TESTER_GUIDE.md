# How to use Marcelorian

Marcelorian helps you write to research labs, one email at a time.
It never sends anything by itself. **You** press Send in Outlook.

You need: a Windows computer, your CV as a PDF, and the lead list file (.csv) from the program owner.

---

## Part 1: Download and start (5 minutes)

1. Open the download link the program owner sent you.
2. Under **Assets**, click the file that ends in **-windows.zip**. Wait for the download to finish.
3. Open your **Downloads** folder.
4. **Right-click** the zip file and choose **Properties**. At the bottom, tick **Unblock**, then click **OK**.
   - If there is no **Unblock** box, skip this step.
5. **Right-click** the zip file again and choose **Extract All…**, then click **Extract**.
   - Do not open the program from inside the zip. Extract it first.
6. Open the new **Marcelorian** folder.
7. Double-click **Start Marcelorian**.
   - If Windows says **"Windows protected your PC"**: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
   - If Windows asks **"Do you want to run this file?"**: click **Run**.
   - If Windows says it **blocked** the file and offers no **Run anyway**: delete the folder, and start again from step 4.
8. A black window opens. **Keep it open** while you use Marcelorian.
9. Your web browser opens Marcelorian by itself.
   - If it does not: click the black window and press **Enter**.

**To stop:** close the black window.
**Next time:** double-click **Start Marcelorian** again. Your work is saved.

---

## Part 2: Practice first

Marcelorian starts in **Practice** mode: nothing reaches a real person. There are four places at the top: **Home**, **Leads**, **Emails** and **Settings**.

### Set up (once, about 5 minutes)

1. Click **Settings** → **Your profile**.
2. Fill in your answers. Press **Save and confirm all** once at the bottom.
   - Add **Your name as on your papers** (for example *Shahriari R*) so PubMed finds your publications.
3. Under **CV**, choose your CV (PDF) and click **Upload CV**.
4. Under **Signature**, set up your signature and click **Save signature**.
5. Under **Your Outlook address**, type the address you send from and click **Save address**.
6. Optional: under **Your publications**, click **Import from PubMed** and mark the ones that are yours.

### Add and check your leads

1. Click **Leads**. Choose your .csv file, click **Check the file**, then **Import these leads**.
   - You can add more files later. People already in your list are recognised and skipped, so nobody is emailed twice.
   - PhD-student positions are hidden.
   - Use the search box, or click a column heading to sort.
2. Click **Check leads one by one**. For each person, paste the page you used and choose what you found (for example **Confirmed current**).
3. Open a checked person and use **Turn into a contact** (salutation, where the email is published, tick the time zone box).

### Write and send a practice email

**Home** shows every person as a card with a progress bar: Lead → Checked → Contact → Drafted → Ready to send → Sent → Replied. Cards move along by themselves. Click a card to do its next step.

1. On **Home**, click **Look up all publications**. It runs by itself; you can keep working.
2. Click a card in **Contact**, then **Draft an email**.
3. The personal lines are **already filled in** from the PI's papers, topics and your profile:
   - To use another suggestion, open the drop-down above a line.
   - To change the email itself, type straight into the email on the right.
4. Click **Approve ✓**. Only a missing address or the do-not-contact list can stop this. Anything else shows as a ⚠ warning.
5. Under **Send** you see a **suggested send time** (the lab's working hours). Nothing is locked.
6. Click **Try it in Outlook (sent to you)** to try the real Outlook link, copy text and email file, all addressed to **you**.
7. Click **Send (practice)** to record it as sent.

### Try the rest

- **Settings → Sending and templates → Practice time:** click **+1 day** or **+1 week** to make follow-ups due sooner.
- After a practice email is sent, open its campaign and try **Simulate a PI reply**, then record what the reply said.
- On a contact's page you can **change or add an email address** at any time.

## Part 3: Real emails (only when the program owner says so)

1. Click **Settings**. Complete the **Before live mode** list.
2. Under **Switch to**, choose **Live**, tick the box, and click **Switch**.
3. Draft and approve an email as you practised.
4. Under **Send**, tick the Outlook check and choose:
   - **Open in Outlook on the web** (best for most people): Outlook opens with the email ready. Attach your CV, check it, then press **Send**. If it isn't the suggested time yet, use **Schedule send** (the arrow next to Send).
   - **Copy the text**: works with any Outlook. Make a new email, paste the text, use the address and subject shown, attach your CV, and press **Send**.
   - **Email file (classic Outlook)**: only for the older Outlook app. It opens ready to send with your CV attached.
5. Come back to Marcelorian and click **I sent it** (or **I did not send it**).

### When a PI replies

1. Read the reply in Outlook.
2. In Marcelorian, open that person's card on **Home**.
3. Under **Record the PI's reply**, choose what the reply said and save. The follow-ups for that lab stop by themselves.

---

## Part 4: Send your progress to the program owner

1. Click **Settings** → **Your data**.
2. Click **owner_report.csv** and **status_summary.csv** to download them.
3. Email both files to the program owner.

---

## If something goes wrong

- **The browser page says it cannot connect:** Marcelorian is closed. Double-click **Start Marcelorian**.
- **"Marcelorian is already running":** use the black window that is already open, and press **Enter** in it.
- **Outlook on the web opens but the email is empty, or shows `+` between words:** sign in to Outlook on the web first, then click the button again. Or use **Copy the text**.
- **A yellow bar at the top:** it lists what needs you now. Click an item to go there.
- **Something else:** take a photo of the screen and the black window, and send it to the program owner.

Your data stays on this computer. To remove everything, go to **Settings → Your data → Delete all my data**.
