# How to use Marcelorian

Marcelorian helps you write to research labs, one email at a time.
It never sends anything by itself. **You** press Send in Outlook.

You need: a Windows computer, your CV as a PDF, and the lead list file (.csv) from the program owner.

---

## Part 1: Download and start (5 minutes)

1. Open the download link the program owner sent you.
2. Under **Assets**, click the file that ends in **-windows.zip**. Wait for the download to finish.
3. Open your **Downloads** folder.
4. **Right-click** the zip file and choose **Properties**. At the bottom, tick **Unblock**, then click **OK**.
   - If there is no **Unblock** box, skip this step.
5. **Right-click** the zip file again and choose **Extract All…**, then click **Extract**.
   - Do not open the program from inside the zip. Extract it first.
6. Open the new **Marcelorian** folder.
7. Double-click **Start Marcelorian**.
   - If Windows says **"Windows protected your PC"**: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
   - If Windows asks **"Do you want to run this file?"**: click **Run**.
   - If Windows says it **blocked** the file and offers no **Run anyway**: delete the folder, and start again from step 4.
8. A black window opens. **Keep it open** while you use Marcelorian.
9. Your web browser opens Marcelorian by itself.
   - If it does not: click the black window and press **Enter**.

**To stop:** close the black window.
**Next time:** double-click **Start Marcelorian** again. Your work is saved.

---

## Part 2: Practice first

Marcelorian starts in **Practice** mode. In practice mode, nothing reaches a real person.
Try everything here first. The top of each page tells you what needs your attention.

### Set up (once)

1. Click **Profile**.
2. Fill in your answers. Click **Save and confirm** on each one.
3. Under **CV**, choose your CV (PDF) and click **Upload CV**.
4. Under **Signature**, set up your signature and click **Save signature**.
5. Under **Your Outlook address**, type the email address you send from and click **Save address**.

### Add your leads

1. Click **Research**.
2. Under **Import a lead list**, choose the .csv file from the program owner.
3. Click **Check the file**, then **Import these leads**.

### Check a lead

1. Click **Verify**.
2. Use the links under **Where to check** to confirm the person and their email.
3. In **Where you checked**, paste the web address of the page you used.
4. Choose what you found (for example **Confirmed current**).
5. Click the person's name at the top to open the lead.
6. Under **Turn into a contact**: check the salutation (for example *Dr. Lee*), choose where the email is published, paste that page's address, and tick **I checked this is the institution's time zone**.
7. Click **Turn into a contact**.

### Write and send a practice email

Every email must be about **this** PI: their research, or a job they posted.

1. Click **Candidates**. Under **Publications for everyone**, click **Look up all**. It runs by itself; you can keep working.
2. Open a contact.
3. If **Interests and posted positions** lists a job, open its page. If it is still open, click **I checked: this posting is current**.
4. Click **Draft an email**.
5. Read **This PI's interests** at the top. Write the personal lines, and mention one of those topics in the line about the PI. In practice you can click **Fill empty lines with practice text**.
6. Click **Save draft and review**.
7. Read the email and the checklist. Click **Approve**.
   - A real email can't be approved until **Tailored to this PI** has a ✓. In practice it only shows ⚠.
8. Click **Send (practice)**.
   - If it says **Not yet**, the lab is outside its working hours. In practice, click **Practice: skip ahead to the send time**.

### Try the rest

- **Settings → Practice time:** click **+1 day** or **+1 week** to make follow-ups due sooner.
- After a practice email is sent, open its campaign and try **Simulate a PI reply**, then record what the reply said.

---

## Part 3: Real emails (only when the program owner says so)

1. Click **Settings**. Complete the **Before live mode** list.
2. Under **Switch to**, choose **Live**, tick the box, and click **Switch**.
3. Draft and approve an email as you practised.
4. When the send button unlocks, choose how to open it:
   - **Open in Outlook on the web** (best for most people): Outlook opens with the email ready. Attach your CV, check it, then press **Send** in Outlook.
   - **Copy the text**: works with any Outlook. Make a new email, paste the text, use the address and subject shown, attach your CV, and press **Send**.
   - **Email file (classic Outlook)**: only for the older Outlook app. It opens ready to send with your CV attached. The new Outlook app may open it without a **Send** button; if so, use **Copy the text**.
5. Come back to Marcelorian and click **I sent it**.
   - If you did not send it, click **I did not send it**.

### When a PI replies

1. Read the reply in Outlook.
2. In Marcelorian, open that campaign.
3. Under **Record the PI's reply**, choose what the reply said and save.
   - Marcelorian stops the follow-ups for that lab by itself.

---

## Part 4: Send your progress to the program owner

1. Click **Settings**, then **Reports for the program owner, backups, and deleting your data**.
2. Click **owner_report.csv** and **status_summary.csv** to download them.
3. Email both files to the program owner.

---

## If something goes wrong

- **The browser page says it cannot connect:** Marcelorian is closed. Double-click **Start Marcelorian**.
- **"Marcelorian is already running":** use the black window that is already open, and press **Enter** in it.
- **Send button is locked:** the email waits for the lab's working hours. The page shows when it unlocks.
- **Outlook on the web opens but the email is empty, or shows `+` between words:** sign in to Outlook on the web first, then click the button again. Or use **Copy the text**.
- **A yellow bar at the top:** it lists what needs you now. Click an item to go there.
- **Something else:** take a photo of the screen and the black window, and send it to the program owner.

Your data stays on this computer. To remove everything, go to **Settings → Your data → Delete all my data**.
